# 03 — High-Converting Video Ad Prompts (3-Second Hook)

Performance video ads live or die in the first **1.5–3.0 seconds**. Instead of generating a single meandering 10-second clip, assemble ads from **3 modular 2.5–3.5 second beats**:
1. **Beat 1 (0–3s)**: Pattern-interrupt visual hook
2. **Beat 2 (3–7s)**: Tactile product proof in real use
3. **Beat 3 (7–10s)**: Clean hero packshot + breathing room for end-card CTA

---

## Beat 1 — Pattern-Interrupt Visual Hook (Editorial High-Contrast)

### Node 2 Keyframe
```text
High-fashion commercial campaign still, 18mm wide-angle lens positioned 35cm from foreground prop. A sleek frosted-glass hydrating serum dropper bottle suspended mid-air above a shallow pool of crystal-clear mirror water against a vivid cobalt-blue architectural plaster wall. Sharp zenith sunlight creates crisp water caustics and gleaming glass refractions. Bold 60-30-10 color balance: cobalt blue, luminous clear glass, and a single crisp amber serum droplet catching the sun.
```

### Node 3 Motion (2.5s Hook, 720p Draft)
```text
High-speed 120fps slow-motion feel. A single luminous amber serum droplet detaches from the glass pipette tip and strikes the mirror-still water below, sending a crisp circular crown splash and shimmering caustics upward. Subtle 8% forward camera push-in, razor-sharp glass refraction, clean commercial pacing.
```

---

## Beat 2 — Tactile Proof / Lifestyle Cut

### Node 2 Keyframe
```text
Sunlit minimalist bathroom vanity still, 50mm prime lens at f/2.0. Natural morning window light casting crisp diagonal blind shadows across pale limestone. A 25-year-old woman with fresh dewy skin gently applies a drop of clear serum to her cheekbone, wearing a crisp white linen robe. Clean authentic skin texture, zero heavy airbrushing, bright airy editorial color grade.
```

### Node 3 Motion (3.0s Proof, 720p Draft)
```text
Gentle handheld micro-drift forward as the subject smoothly glides two fingertips upward along her cheekbone. Natural morning sunlight catches a subtle dewy sheen on skin and glass bottle edge. Relaxed natural blink and faint smile, realistic skin physics, zero morphing.
```

---

## Beat 3 — Clean Packshot with CTA Safe Zone

### Node 2 Keyframe
```text
Clean luxury e-commerce hero packshot, 85mm studio lens. Frosted-glass serum bottle standing upright on the left third of a wet pale limestone slab, leaving generous uncluttered negative space on the right half for typography overlay. Crisp directional backlight illuminating translucent glass edges, minimal water droplets on stone.
```

### Node 3 Motion (3.0s Packshot, 720p Draft)
```text
Ultra-smooth 5% lateral slider glide to the right. Subtle sunlight caustics ripple gently across the wet stone surface behind the bottle while the frosted glass bottle stays completely still and razor-sharp. Clean uncluttered right-side negative space preserved throughout.
```
